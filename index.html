Who we are

Rootline is a small team of independent security researchers — not a company, not an agency. The people who scope your test are the people who run it.

We work across web applications, APIs, mobile apps, cloud environments and AI systems. Every engagement is manual testing against real attack paths: broken access control, business logic flaws, authentication and session issues, injection, misconfiguration — the things automated scans miss.

Our clients are small companies and startups. They get the same depth a large firm would sell them, at a price they can actually pay, with a report their engineers can act on.

How an engagement works

01

Scope in writing

We agree the targets, the window and what is off-limits — in writing, before anything is touched.

02

Test like an attacker

Manual testing against your live scope, the way a real attacker would approach it. No scan-and-send reports.

03

Report with proof

Every finding comes with reproduction steps, impact in plain language, and what to fix first.

04

Retest & attest

We retest critical fixes for free and sign an attestation letter for your customers or auditors.

Rules we never break: nothing is tested without written authorization from the owner of the system · third-party payment, banking and partner systems are out of scope unless explicitly agreed · findings are reported only to you, never shared or published.
